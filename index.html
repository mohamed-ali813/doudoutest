Who I Am

Hi, I'm Doudou
Creative Designer.

A graphic designer who loves turning ideas into visuals that actually feel alive. I design social media posts, print materials, logos, and UI/UX interfaces with a clean and modern style, always trying to make every project look unique instead of "just another design".

I enjoy mixing creativity with simplicity, whether it's building a brand identity, designing an Instagram post, or creating an interface that feels smooth and easy to use.
